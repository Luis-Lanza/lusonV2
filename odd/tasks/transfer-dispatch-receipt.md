## Business acceptance criteria
1. Only the physical origin can dispatch an approved request, in full.
2. Dispatch atomically deducts each item's physical existing balance and its reserved balance by the same quantity, consuming only this request's allocation. Unreserved availability is unchanged; other holds survive.
3. No destination inventory credit occurs at dispatch; destination receives later.
4. Original immutable request items and approval metadata remain intact. Record server-derived dispatch actor/time and in-transit outcome; retries never deduct twice.
5. Participant list/detail/creation replay and decision retries handle the later state coherently without spurious unavailable responses.
6. Existing commit-only inventory/status invalidations are reused; rollback and retries emit no new mutation events.
7. Receipt is destination-only and finalized once. Expected available units are `min(actual, sent)` per expected variant. Persist shortages, excess and unexpected catalog variants separately; exceptions do not become automatically sale-available. User permits unexpected-only arrivals (positive total actual units, zero expected arrivals); capture all expected shortages truthfully.
8. No total-zero receipt, second receipt, automatic loss/return or discrepancy-resolution workflow.
9. Preserve location/owner fencing and explicit uncertain-result recovery in the frontend; never silently replay uncertain mutations.
10. User selected `counters_with_receipt`: existing counters remain explicitly approval-only through D2. With receipt completion, expand once to all location-owned required approvals, dispatches and receipts; never count unread events or another location's obligations.

## Receipt product decision
- User explicitly selected `allow_unexpected_only`: permit a one-time receipt with zero expected units received and positive unexpected catalog units. Record all sent units as shortages and unexpected arrivals separately; neither shortages nor unexpected units trigger automatic loss/return/resolution or sale availability. Total-zero actual receipts remain forbidden.
