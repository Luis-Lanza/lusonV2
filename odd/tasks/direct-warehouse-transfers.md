## Settled business rules

- Warehouse creation and physical dispatch are separate actions.
- Creation reserves the complete immutable shipment from warehouse availability, without changing physical stock or crediting the destination.
- No destination approval is required.
- Warehouse is original creator and physical origin; destination is the selected branch.
- Warehouse may cancel before dispatch, releasing only its own reservations. No cancellation after dispatch.
- Dispatch consumes this shipment's reservation and moves physical units to transit; destination subsequently records the existing single immutable receipt.
- Do not invent a later human approval event for a shipment authorized/reserved at creation. History and UI must communicate the distinction from branch-request approval truthfully.
