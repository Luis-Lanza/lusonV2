## UX direction to prototype, not yet approved
1. General detail shell: reference, status, compact route, products and only the relevant authorized next action. No oversized metric dashboard, always-expanded sidebars, repeated prose or stacks of every workflow. Received/rejected/cancelled screens must not invent a universal primary business action.
2. Received products show verified current effective count when complete/current, with historical original receipt accessible separately. Receipt credit and its difference from current stock remain clear where relevant (e.g. correction review), not another dominant metrics wall. Shortages and excesses stay variant-specific, never netted across variants; unexpected variants retain zero-credit policy. Never bring the original historical shortage back as the apparent current outcome.
3. Contextual row actions and focused discrepancy/count/restitution dialogs; do not stack every editable form, uncertainty record and history on the main page.
4. Preserve explicit edit -> review -> confirm steps. Put technical operation/revision evidence in discoverable disclosures, without hiding material stock effects, reasons, actor/location or decision/application history.
5. Busy, unavailable, masked, incomplete, duplicate, inconsistent, pending or uncertain evidence cannot display invented zeros or an unqualified resolved status. Current effective values come from confirmed complete evidence, not proposed quantities.
6. Preserve existing role/account permissions: count participants/counterparty head-revision decisions; restitution origin-account proposal, destination-account decision and unchecked physical-retention attestation. Restitution does not change destination/reservations.
7. Native modal requirements: keyboard focus across inputs/selects/checkbox/textarea/buttons, background inertness, Escape where safe, explicit cancel, trigger restoration including disabled/removed trigger fallback, stale-context review retirement. No silent rebasing of a decision.
8. Spanish UI copy, existing wine/off-white/Inter visual language, clear desktop/mobile hierarchy. Exact source tokens: wine `#7A1F3D`, hover `#5B162D`, tint `#F5E8ED`, canvas `#F8F6F4`, white surfaces, text `#242124`, muted `#6B6267`, border `#DED7DA`, Inter/base14px, cards6px, buttons4px, action targets>=44px. No navy banner or arbitrary branding. Products use brand/model/Ah/polarity, no invented SKU. Direct received shipments use "Envío recibido", not "Solicitud recibida". Persisted location determines history actor, not current viewer.
9. Reuse existing feature-local modal visual/focus foundations. Lifecycle actions retain current eligibility: pending approver approves/rejects; approved origin dispatches; in-transit destination receives; creator may cancel pending/approved. Moving an action into a modal never broadens permission or changes business sequencing.
